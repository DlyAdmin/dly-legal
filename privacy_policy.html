Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dly ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Android application.

1. Local Planning and Attached Files

Core planning does not require a Dly account. Dly creates a local profile and stores the tasks, notes, categories, habits, completion history, recorded time and preferences you enter in app storage on your device.

When you attach a file, Dly stores its name, type, size and a private copy of its contents. The system picker grants access to the files you select; Dly does not browse your media library or documents in the background. Your original files are not changed or deleted.

Local-first does not mean that no data leaves your device. Advertising, purchases, optional Google Drive sync, speech recognition and files you choose to export or share involve the services described below.

2. Optional Google Drive Sync

If you connect Google Drive, Google handles authentication. Dly stores the connected email address and authorization credentials on your device so it can transfer your backup. Dly does not receive your Google password.

Sync uses Google Drive's hidden app-data folder, rather than access to all of your Drive files. It uploads and downloads your planning backup, including attached files, and can run automatically while you remain signed in with sync enabled.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

Dly displays ads served by Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK may collect and share advertising/device identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google's Privacy Policy applies.

Available advertising-ID and ad-personalization controls are in your Android and Google privacy settings. Controls vary by device and Android version.

4. Subscriptions – Google Play

Dly offers an optional monthly Remove Ads subscription through Google Play. Ads are removed only while an eligible subscription is active. Google Play shows the price, billing period and renewal terms before purchase and lets you manage or cancel the subscription.

Google processes payments. Dly receives purchase and subscription-status information to provide and restore access; Dly does not receive or store your payment-card or bank details. Google's Payments Privacy Notice describes its payment data handling.

5. Voice Input, Exports and Sharing

Voice quick add requests microphone access and uses your device's speech recognition provider. The provider may process audio online, depending on your device and settings. Dly uses the recognized text for the command you review. The provider's privacy terms apply.

Exporting a backup, opening an attachment or sharing files transfers copies to the destination you choose. Those apps and services handle the copies under their own privacy policies. Backups and sync include attached file contents.

Dly does not run a server that stores your planning database and does not sell your planning files. Google and other selected providers process data as described above.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Deleting items in Dly removes their local records. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local app data on that device. Device/platform backups, exports, shared copies and provider-retained data are separate and may persist.

Removing an attachment removes its reference. Bodies still referenced by another task or Backlog item are retained; unreferenced bodies are cleaned up on a subsequent app launch. Temporary open/share caches are managed by the operating system. Original files are not deleted.

While sync is enabled, deletions can propagate to the synced backup. Signing out or uninstalling does not itself delete the Google Drive backup. Use Google Drive's app-management controls to delete Dly's hidden app data and your Google Account's connections controls to revoke access. Delete exported or shared copies separately.

7. Children's Privacy

Dly is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Security

Keep your device and Google account secure and protect exported files. Backups can contain your planning information and attached file contents. Dly cannot control the security or retention of copies held by other apps and service providers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app or on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
